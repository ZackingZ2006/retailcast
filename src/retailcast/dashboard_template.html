02 / Validate4 × 28-day expanding windows. Training labels are purged at every origin.
03 / Calibrate2 × 28-day windows. Per-market absolute-error intervals; no test residuals used.
04 / TestOne untouched 28-day window. Algorithms stay locked when refitted on all history.
Interpretation matters. Sales are observed transactions, not unconstrained demand. Intervals are empirical: temporal dependence and seasonal shifts can reduce coverage. The continuation follows the end of the historical dataset; it is not a forecast for the present day. Daily interval bounds must not be added to claim coverage for 28-day totals.
